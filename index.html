The beginning

The Day Our Names Met

Hope Primary School · Kakuma

It all started at Hope Primary School in Kakuma, Kenya.

One day, I was passing by the library with my friend Lukaka. I decided to write my name, THIJIN VS, and then I walked away.

What I didn't know was that someone special was watching me from a distance. It was you, Rosalia. You were curious about what I was doing. After I left, you came closer and wrote your name, ROSALIA, just underneath mine.

Maybe it looked like a small thing at the time, but today it means so much to me. Two names written in one place became the beginning of a beautiful memory.

THIJIN VS The first name ❤️

Across the distance

When Distance Became Part of Our Story

2020 · Kakuma to Egypt

In 2020, life took us in different directions. You left for Egypt, while I remained in Kakuma, Kenya.

Suddenly, the places that once connected us were far apart. We could no longer share everyday moments in the same way. Distance became part of our story, and time moved on.

Sometimes I wonder what is in your heart today. I don't know whether you still see me as your boyfriend or whether you still feel the same way I do.

But I want you to know something: my feelings for you are still real.

🇰🇪 Kakuma Scholes

My Forever Message to You

Rosalia, even though we are miles apart, I still treasure the memories we created.

I cannot promise that life will always be easy, and I cannot decide what the future holds for both of us. But I can honestly tell you how I feel.

For me, you are still my forever. ❤️

I don't know what tomorrow will bring, but I will always be grateful that our paths crossed at Hope Primary School.

If I could go back to that day beside the library, I would still write my name, hoping that one day you would write yours beneath it again.

With all my love,
